Learning Objectives

  • Explain the neuroscience of emerging adulthood (Prefrontal Cortex, Dopamine System).
  • Define the "Six Pillars" and how they align with our organizational philosophy.
  • Describe systemic challenges for youth aging out of foster care in Texas.
  • Apply trauma-informed and strengths-based care principles.
  • Identify mandatory reporting obligations under Texas Family Code Chapter 261.

Key Concepts: The Developing Brain

Neuroplasticity as Opportunity: The program acts as a direct intervention to build new neural pathways.

The Dopamine System: Understanding the "engine of exploration" that drives behavior in young adults.

The Prefrontal Cortex (PFC): Often called the "Command Center," the PFC is responsible for executive functions like decision-making and impulse control, and is the primary focus of our developmental support.
