# Six Pillar Program: Core Certification Guidelines

**Texas Choice Solutions Inc.**
**Supervised Independent Living Program**
**A Comprehensive Training Curriculum for SIL Counselors**

**Professional Philosophy: Brain-Builders for Independence**
This framework is science-based, designed to support young adults transitioning out of foster care by focusing on developmental milestones such as emotional regulation, identity formation, and social belonging.

**Program Architecture: 10-Day Intensive Module Focus**
- **Day 1-2 (Module 1):** Foundations of the Six Pillars Framework (Science and Ethics).
- **Day 3-4 (Module 2):** Pillar Deep Dives (Practical implementation of the six specific pillars).
- **Day 5-6 (Module 3):** Documentation, Assessment, and Quality Assurance.
- **Day 7-9 (Module 4):** Practicum & Simulations (Real-world application and role-play).
- **Day 10:** Final Certification Exam and Review.
