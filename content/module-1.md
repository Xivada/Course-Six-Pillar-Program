# Module 1: Foundations of the Six Pillars Framework

**Texas Choice Solutions Inc.**
**Training Days 1 & 2 | 12 Contact Hours**

## Introduction
Establishes a scientific and ethical foundation for the "Six Pillars" framework, emphasizing that the brain remains "under construction" until age 25.

## Learning Objectives
1. Explain the neuroscience of emerging adulthood (Prefrontal Cortex, Dopamine System).
2. Define the "Six Pillars" and alignment with TCS philosophy.
3. Describe systemic challenges for youth aging out of foster care in Texas.
4. Apply trauma-informed and strengths-based care.
5. Demonstrate culturally responsive engagement.
6. Identify mandatory reporting obligations (Texas Family Code Chapter 261).
7. Articulate professional boundary expectations.

## Key Concepts
- **Neuroplasticity as Opportunity**: Direct intervention to build new neural pathways.
- **The Dopamine System**: The "engine of exploration" in young adults.
- **The Prefrontal Cortex**: The "Command Center" still developing during SIL transition.
