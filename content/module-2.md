# Module 2: Pillar Deep Dives

**Texas Choice Solutions Inc.**
**Training Days 3-8 | 36 Contact Hours**

## Overview
Operational core of the certification course. Counselors master the skills required to deliver the six specific pillars.

## The Six Pillars
1. **Stability**
2. **Connection**
3. **Purpose**
3. **Identity**
4. **Resilience**
5. **Contribution**

## Training Structure (Per Pillar)
- **Science and Theory**: Deep dive into neuroplasticity and PFC development.
- **Session Delivery Practice**: Rehearsal of 24 session plans.
- **Peer Facilitation Lab**: Facilitation practice with feedback via the Quality Rubric.

## Key Tools
- **The Curiosity Map**: Collaborative visual tool for thematic growth.
- **The PAUSE Framework**: (Prepare, Assess, Understand, Select, Evaluate) - Core decision-making tool.
- **Identity Mosaic & Strengths Spotlight**: Tools for visualizing self-concept.
