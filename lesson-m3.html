Documentation & Quality Assurance

Professional Accountability

This module provides counselors with the standards required to track progress accurately and maintain high quality of care.

The SOAR Progress Note

The SOAR format is our standard for all session documentation:

  • S (Status): Current state of the resident.
  • O (Observation): Objective data and behaviors during the session.
  • A (Analysis): Interpretation of observations through the Six Pillars lens.
  • R (Response): Counselor's intervention and plan for follow-up.

Quality Standards

Curiosity Map Data Entry: Translating visual notes into structured organizational data.

Quality Circles: Regular peer-review sessions where documentation is evaluated for clarity and accuracy.

Incident Reporting: Trauma-informed precision in documenting safety concerns.
