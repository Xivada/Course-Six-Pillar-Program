Training Day 10

Practicum & Certification

Final Evaluation

Day 10 is dedicated to demonstrating mastery of the Six Pillars framework through simulation and written assessment.

The Practicum Simulation

A 30-minute live session with a participant. You will select a random profile scenario and must demonstrate:

  • Trauma-Informed Awareness
  • Facilitation Flow
  • Mastery of Framework Tools
  • Maintenance of Professional Boundaries

The Written Exam

The written exam covers theoretical knowledge, Texas Family Code compliance, and a reflection on your own facilitation style.
