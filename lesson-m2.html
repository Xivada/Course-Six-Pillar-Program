Training Days 3-8

Pillar Deep Dives

The Operational Core

Across six intensive training days, counselors transition from understanding the framework conceptually to mastering the skills required to deliver it in practice.

The Six Specific Pillars

  • Stability: Creating a foundation of safety and predictability.
  • Connection: Building healthy, supportive relationships.
  • Purpose: Finding meaning and direction in daily life.
  • Identity: Developing a positive self-concept.
  • Resilience: Navigating setbacks with adaptive strategies.
  • Contribution: Engaging with the community in meaningful ways.

Core Tools

The PAUSE Framework: A five-step decision-making tool: Prepare, Assess, Understand, Select, and Evaluate.

The Curiosity Map: A collaborative tool used to track thematic growth and identify areas for deeper exploration.

Identity Mosaic: Helps residents visualize their multifaceted identity and inherent capabilities.
